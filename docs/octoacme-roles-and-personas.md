# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Testing Leads ensure that delivered features meet quality standards and acceptance criteria. They own test strategy, quality assurance processes, and validation workflows.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans and quality acceptance criteria in collaboration with Product and Development
- Build and maintain automated test suites (unit, integration, end-to-end)
- Execute manual QA and user acceptance testing
- Identify and triage quality issues, coordinate fixes with developers
- Validate release readiness and sign-off on production deployments
- Participate in sprint planning to estimate testing effort
- Report quality metrics and trends to leadership

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Maintain high test coverage and automation ROI
- Reduce time spent on manual testing through effective automation
- Ensure features are usable and meet customer expectations

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and retrospectives
- Daily standups (status on test execution, blockers)
- QA sign-off gates before merging and deployment
- Metrics dashboards shared with Product and PM leads

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Review acceptance criteria, provide test feedback, collaborate on test design
- **Product Manager**: Refine acceptance criteria, validate feature completeness
- **Project Manager**: Ensure testing timeline and resource plans align with delivery schedule
- **Technical Lead**: Collaborate on testability and performance requirements
- **Product Lead**: Report quality metrics and escalate critical issues

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads own the technical vision, architecture decisions, and technical risk management for projects. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with system design principles.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design for project features
- Review designs and code for adherence to standards and best practices
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and mitigation strategies
- Mentor developers and support technical problem-solving
- Participate in estimation and capacity planning
- Support performance, security, and scalability validation
- Represent technical concerns in cross-team discussions

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Minimize technical debt and rework
- Ensure security and performance standards are met
- Build team technical capability and shared ownership

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Sprint planning and retrospectives
- Risk registers and technical dependency mapping
- Code review leadership and mentoring

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide technical guidance, review designs and code
- **Product Manager**: Advise on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- **Project Manager**: Highlight technical risks and dependencies affecting timeline
- **QA Lead**: Collaborate on testability and performance requirements

---

## Product Operations / Analytics

### Role Summary
Product Operations and Analytics professionals track metrics, measure success criteria, and support data-driven decision-making. They bridge product vision with measurable outcomes and provide insights for continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics and KPIs aligned with project goals
- Set up dashboards and monitoring for key signals (errors, latency, usage, adoption)
- Analyze feature performance and user behavior data
- Provide insights and recommendations to Product and Project teams
- Track velocity, burndown, and project health metrics
- Support A/B testing and experimentation workflows
- Generate reports for stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Enable data-driven decision-making throughout the project lifecycle
- Establish a culture of measurement and continuous improvement
- Identify opportunities to optimize product and delivery processes
- Provide transparency on project health and outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Weekly metrics reviews with Product and PM leads
- Dashboard updates and trend analysis
- Stakeholder reports and executive briefings
- Post-release impact assessment

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Collaborate on success metrics and KPI definitions
- **Project Manager**: Provide velocity and project health metrics
- **Developers**: Share performance and quality metrics
- **QA Lead**: Monitor quality-related KPIs and test coverage

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove process blockers, and coach teams on continuous improvement. They ensure the team follows defined processes and maintains a sustainable pace.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove blockers to team productivity
- Coach team on agile practices and principles
- Maintain and optimize the sprint workflow and project board
- Support retrospective action items and process improvements
- Escalate impediments that require management attention
- Track team health and engagement signals

### Goals
- Maximize team productivity and sustainable delivery pace
- Foster continuous improvement and psychological safety
- Ensure transparency in project execution
- Reduce cycle time and improve predictability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-ones with team members for coaching
- Retrospective facilitation and action tracking
- Process improvement recommendations to Product and Project Leads

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Remove blockers, coach on sprint discipline
- **Project Manager**: Escalate impediments and report team health
- **Product Manager**: Ensure backlog is ready for sprint planning
- **All Roles**: Facilitate transparent communication and collaboration

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors represent business interests, provide strategic direction, and ensure alignment with organizational goals. They approve funding, scope, and major decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success criteria
- Provide governance and approval gates (initiation, planning, release)
- Communicate project priorities and organizational context
- Escalate issues that impact budget, timeline, or scope
- Support stakeholder communication and change management
- Review progress and outcomes against business objectives

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy
- Minimize business risk and stakeholder surprises
- Enable timely decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and milestone reviews
- Executive steering committee meetings (if applicable)
- Status updates and risk escalations
- Post-release business impact assessment

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Manager**: Receive status updates, approve scope changes
- **Product Manager**: Provide business context and priorities
- **Leadership**: Escalation point for critical business issues

---

## Cross-Functional Partner (Security, Compliance, Support, etc.)

### Role Summary
Cross-functional partners from specialized domains (Security, Compliance, Support, DevOps) represent critical concerns and integration points. They ensure projects meet domain-specific standards and enable smooth handoffs.

### Responsibilities
- Define domain-specific requirements and standards (security, compliance, support readiness)
- Review designs and implementations for domain alignment
- Identify and mitigate domain-specific risks
- Support training and documentation for domain concerns
- Participate in go/no-go decisions related to their domain
- Enable knowledge transfer and operational readiness

### Goals
- Ensure projects meet all regulatory and operational standards
- Prevent security, compliance, or operational issues in production
- Enable smooth handoff to support and operations teams
- Build shared ownership across domains

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and risk assessments
- Integration planning and testing participation
- Pre-release readiness checklists and sign-offs
- Post-release support and incident response

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Technical Lead**: Collaborate on architecture and design reviews
- **QA Lead**: Coordinate on domain-specific testing (security, compliance)
- **Project Manager**: Escalate domain-specific risks and dependencies
- **Product Manager**: Advise on domain trade-offs and feasibility
